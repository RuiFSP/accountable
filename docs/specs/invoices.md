# Spec: Invoices

- **Invoices**: the bills the business sends for the deals it has won, and where the payments land. Under Money, the Invoices tab lists every invoice with its status and what is still owed. Open one to see the document the customer gets, the payments recorded against it and its PDF. Each deal and each organization shows the invoices that belong to it.
- **Invoice**: the bill for one won deal. Its number (given when it is issued), the deal and organization it is for, the issue and due dates, its lines, the subtotal, the tax from Settings with its label and rate, and the total. Its Bill to block names the organization, its billing address and the deal's contact; its letterhead carries the business's own details from Settings. A line keeps the product's name and price as they were when it was added, with a quantity and a description. Every amount is whole cents and shows in US dollars with two decimals, such as `$1,234.56`.
- **Making one**: from a won deal you pick products, set quantities and adjust any line's description or price. The lines must add up to the deal's value before tax; the form shows what is still to allocate. A live preview beside the form shows the document exactly as the customer will see it. A draft can be edited or deleted as often as you like, and a won deal has at most one invoice that isn't void.
- **Issuing**: issuing takes the invoice number — the prefix from Settings followed by its sequence, such as `INV-0001`, with no gaps or repeats — and sets the due date from the payment terms in Settings. The issue date is chosen when you issue, defaulting to today. From then on the invoice is fixed: nothing on it changes, and a mistake is fixed by voiding it, which keeps its number and marks the document VOID. An invoice with payments can't be voided; delete its payments first. A voided invoice is kept for the record and doesn't stop a replacement.
- **Payments**: payments arrive against an issued invoice, in full or in part. Each has a date, an amount, a method — bank transfer, card, check, cash or other — and an optional reference. The invoice shows a bar of how much is paid and what is still owed. A payment entered by mistake is deleted and the balance goes back up. An invoice whose balance reaches zero is paid; one still owed after its due date is overdue.
- **The PDF**: every issued invoice downloads as a PDF that looks like the preview, with its paid, overdue or void mark on it. Whatever is typed onto an invoice appears there as plain text, never as markup.

## Design decisions

Points this spec fixes:

1. **One invoice per won deal, made from the deal.** An invoice is created on a won deal's page and nowhere else, so the Invoices tab has no Add. A won deal has at most one invoice that isn't void, and the database enforces it as well as the API, so two people creating one at the same moment cannot both win. The deal keeps its value while a non-void invoice exists: the edit dialog locks the value and the API refuses a change with a 409. Deleting a draft or voiding the invoice frees the value again. The invoice is only created for a won deal, and once made it keeps its deal and its organization whatever the deal becomes.
2. **The lines make the deal's value.** A new invoice starts with one line carrying the whole deal's value, and you replace it with product lines as you like, as long as they add up to the same subtotal. The form shows the deal's value, the subtotal and what is still to allocate, and won't save while the two differ. Lines come from active products and keep the product's name and price as they were added; a line's description and price can be edited, and a product's later rename or price change never touches it. A quantity is a whole number from 1 to 1,000,000 and every amount stays within the money columns' limit.
3. **Draft, issue, void.** A draft has no number and can be changed or deleted. Issuing takes the number and freezes what is on the invoice: its lines, its tax label and rate, its totals and its Bill to block are the ones stored at issue, and later changes to Settings, the organization or the contact never move them. Void marks the document VOID and keeps the number; only an invoice with no payments can be voided. Only a draft can be deleted, and deleting it takes its lines with it.
4. **The status is what the balance says.** An invoice stores whether it is a draft, issued or void; the rest is worked out: part paid when some of the total is paid, paid when the balance is zero, overdue when money is still owed after the due date. One status shows at a time — void and draft first, then paid, overdue, part paid, issued — so an invoice past its due date with money in reads as overdue. Due today is not overdue. The paid, overdue and void marks name their status on the preview and the PDF; no state is ever shown by color alone.
5. **The backend does the sums.** A line's amount is its quantity times its unit price, the subtotal adds the lines, the tax is worked out once on the subtotal at the settings' rate to the nearest cent with halves up, and the total is the two together. The backend works out every amount and balance and never trusts totals sent from the browser. A draft's tax follows the current settings while it is a draft; issuing stores the label and rate with the invoice.
6. **Payments.** A payment is more than zero and no more than what is still owed, is dated when it arrived — today by default, and never a date in the future — and records who entered it. Payments are taken only against an issued invoice: a draft or a void invoice takes none. Deleting a payment asks first and puts the balance back up.
7. **The Bill to block.** The organization's name and billing address and the deal's contact, read when the invoice is issued and kept as they were. A draft shows the organization and contact as they are now. With no billing address the block shows the name and contact alone.
8. **The three designs.** An invoice is drawn in one of the three designs chosen in Settings — Classic, Modern or Compact — with the business's accent color and logo, on the brand's fonts: Instrument Sans for the words, JetBrains Mono for the numbers and identifiers. US Letter, portrait, readable in black and white as well as in color. The accent is used on the document only, never in the app around it. The design, accent and logo are the team's current choice and apply to every invoice's preview and PDF, issued ones included; only what is on the invoice is frozen at issue.
9. **The PDF is made when you download it.** It is drawn with a PDF library that installs with uv and runs on Render's native Python runtime with no system packages, using the same bundled brand fonts the app uses. Every string typed onto an invoice is drawn as text, so markup never reaches the document. A void invoice's PDF carries its VOID mark.
10. **The list and the panels.** The Invoices tab has columns for number, organization, deal, issue date, due date, total, balance and status, with search over number, organization and deal, sorting, and a status filter. A draft shows no number and no balance; a void invoice shows no balance. The deal's panel offers Create invoice on a won deal with no non-void invoice and lists every invoice of the deal, void ones included; the organization's panel lists every invoice of the organization. Both open the invoice's page.
