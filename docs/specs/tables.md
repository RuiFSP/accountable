# Spec: Tables

Every record table has a search box, columns you can sort by clicking their headings, a way to add a new record, and edit and delete on each row. That's how a new deal, a new lead, a new customer, a new product or a new bill is added. Deleting asks first, and says what will happen to the records linked to the one being deleted. Deletion cascades: deleting an organization deletes its contacts and deals, and deleting a contact or deal deletes its activities — the confirmation lists everything that will go. The Products table is the exception: products are archived and restored, never deleted, as [Products](products.md) describes, and a Show archived control brings the hidden ones back into view. The Quotes list is another: a quote is a document, never deleted, and its list opens each quote instead of editing a row, as [Quotes](quotes.md) describes. The invoices table is another: an invoice is made on a won deal's page rather than added here, a draft is edited or deleted and an issued invoice only voided or downloaded, as [Invoices](invoices.md) describes. Search works at two levels: each table's own box searches that table's records, and a box in the top bar finds any organization, contact, deal or active product by part of its name, ignoring case — the same on SQLite and Postgres.

## Design decisions

Points this spec fixes:

6. **The cascade confirmation scrolls.** The delete dialog lists every record and activity that will go; with sample data an organization can take a dozen rows. The dialog scrolls, never truncates the list.
10. **Two searches.** The top bar's search matches part of an organization, contact, deal or active product name, ignoring case, the same on SQLite and Postgres. Each table's own search box keeps working the way it does today; a product match opens the Products page with that product shown, and archived products are left out of the top bar. Bills are found with the Bills table's own box, which matches the supplier's name and the bill number; quotes and invoices are searched from their own tabs. They are all left out of the top bar.
11. **A record another area holds is not deleted.** A deal or an organization with a quote, invoice or bill pointing at it is refused with a 409, and the dialog names what holds it and offers only Close. A quote holds the deal it belongs to, and an organization is held by the quotes on any of its deals; a bill holds its supplier organization. Invoices hold their deal and their organization, void ones included. Contacts and activities are never held, and a bill itself holds nothing, so deleting a bill always goes through.
12. **The invoices table keeps the table rules, minus Add.** Its own search box matches part of a number, an organization or a deal, ignoring case, the same on SQLite and Postgres; the columns sort and a status filter stands where the status filters stand on the other tables. There is no Add, because an invoice starts on a won deal. A draft offers Edit and Delete, and its delete confirmation names the lines that go with it; an issued invoice offers Void and Download PDF and nothing that changes it.
