# Spec: Quotes

- **Quotes**: the offers the business sends to its customers. A quote belongs to one deal and offers products and prices to that deal's customer. The Quotes tab of Money lists every quote, each row its number, deal and organization, the state it is in, its valid-until date and its total including tax, with five totals across the top. A deal's page and an organization's page each list the quotes that belong to them. Open a quote to see it as the customer will see it, mark it sent, accepted or declined, and download its PDF.
- **Quote**: a document on one deal. Its number, such as QT-0001, its valid-until date, its lines, the tax label and rate, and the subtotal, tax and total. It is a draft, sent, expired, accepted or declined. Its page shows who added it and who last changed it, as their email. A quote is never deleted: a draft is edited, and a quote that is no longer a draft is copied to a new one.
- **The lines**: each line holds a product's name and unit price as they were when the line was added, the product's description as its starting text, a quantity and an amount. Only active products can be added to a quote, and a line stays when its product is archived. The description, the quantity and the unit price can be changed on the line. The amount is the quantity times the unit price, the subtotal adds the lines, the tax is worked out once on the subtotal at the settings' rate, and the total is the two together. The backend works out every amount and never trusts totals sent from the browser.
- **The customer**: the quote shows the deal's organization and, when the deal has one, its main contact, together with the company's details from Settings.
- **The states**: a quote starts as a draft and takes its number when it is created, from an open deal. Only a draft changes. Marking one sent stores the tax label and rate and freezes the quote; after that nothing about it changes. A sent quote shows as expired once its valid-until date has passed, and can still be marked accepted or declined. Accepted and declined are final. A sent or expired quote is changed by copying it to a new draft, which starts with a new number, the same lines and a fresh validity.
- **The validity**: a quote is valid for 30 days from the day it is created, unless you change the date while it is a draft. The date is shown on the quote and on its PDF.
- **The PDF**: the quote page offers Download PDF once the quote is sent. The PDF is made when you download it, from the quote's saved values, and looks like the preview: the company's details, the customer, the number and dates, the lines and the totals. Anything typed into the quote appears on it as plain text, never as markup.
- **Accepting**: marking a quote accepted moves its deal to Won and sets the deal's value to the quote's subtotal, before tax, rounded to the nearest dollar. A deal has at most one accepted quote. Marking a quote declined changes only the quote.

## Design decisions

Points this spec fixes:

1. **A quote starts from its deal.** A quote is created from its deal's page, so the Quotes tab has no New quote action: its rows open a quote and its five totals read across the top. The deal's panel lists its quotes — number, state, valid-until date and total — and offers New quote while the deal is open, in New, Qualified, Proposal or Negotiation. A Won or Lost deal keeps the quotes it already has and takes no new ones. The organization's panel lists the same for the quotes of its deals, adding the deal each one belongs to. Both panels' rows open the quote.
2. **Expired is a view, not a stored state.** The stored states are draft, sent, accepted and declined. Once a sent quote's valid-until date has passed, the quote shows and totals as expired, and it can still be marked accepted or declined. The date counts through its last day: a quote valid until Nov 30 is expired on Dec 1.
3. **Drafts follow the settings; sending freezes the tax.** A draft's tax label and rate come from the settings as they are, so changing the settings moves its totals. Marking the quote sent stores the label and rate, and the quote keeps them for good. A line keeps the product's name and price as they were when the line was added, so a later change to the product changes nothing on the quote.
4. **The preview is the customer's document.** The form shows the live preview beside it, and the preview and the PDF share one layout: the company's details from Settings, the customer, the number and the dates, the lines, the subtotal, the tax with its label and rate, and the total. The document is white with Midnight ink in both app themes — it is the customer's paper, not the app's — and follows the brand's fonts and money style: Instrument Sans for the text, Instrument Serif for the document's title, JetBrains Mono for the number and every amount, and money always with its currency.
5. **The PDF is made on the server when it is downloaded.** It is generated from the quote's saved values, never from what the browser sends, and anything typed into the quote appears as plain text, never as markup. It is made with a pure-Python library that installs with uv and needs no system packages on Render. A draft is previewed on screen and downloaded once it is sent.
6. **Accepting a quote is a board move.** Marking a quote accepted moves its deal to Won by the same rules as dragging the card there — the new stage's default probability, its place at the end of the Won column, and the system timeline entry naming who moved it — and sets the deal's value to the quote's subtotal before tax, rounded to the nearest dollar, halves up. A deal has at most one accepted quote, so accepting a second one is refused; an expired quote can still be accepted.
7. **Nothing is deleted.** Quotes have no delete action and no delete route. A wrong draft is edited instead of removed, and a sent or expired quote is copied to a new draft. The copy keeps the deal and the lines, takes a new number and a fresh 30-day validity, and leaves the quote it came from as it is.
8. **Numbers never gap or repeat.** The number is the quote prefix from Settings followed by the next count to at least four figures — QT-0001, then QT-0002 — given when the quote is created and taken inside the transaction that saves it, so a failed save leaves no gap and two saves at the same moment cannot repeat one. It follows [Company settings](company-settings.md) decision 19.
9. **The five totals.** The Quotes tab opens with Draft, Sent, Expired, Accepted and Declined, each the total value of the quotes in that state, including tax. A draft's total follows the tax rate in the settings now, and a sent, expired, accepted or declined quote's follows the rate it kept when it was sent. Each quote counts once, in the state it shows.
